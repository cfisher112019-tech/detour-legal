Privacy Policy

Detour, by C FISH ENTERPRISES LLC. Last updated 18 October 2026.

Detour is a counter. It records the times you tap a button and the answers you gave when you set it up. That is all it is for, and this page says plainly what happens to that information.

What Detour keeps

  • Your taps. The time of each craving you log, and whether you marked it beaten or a slip.
  • Your answers. How many times a week you said you eat the habit, what an order costs you, your reason, the times of day you chose, the word you use for the habit, and the date you started.
  • How the app is used, counted anonymously. Detour records a short list of things that happen — a screen in setup being reached, a craving logged, a craving beaten or an order logged, a nightly check-in answered, a subscription starting or ending, the app being opened — each with the time it happened. These are attached to a random identifier created on your phone the first time the app runs. It identifies that installation of the app and nothing else: it is not an advertising identifier, it is not shared with anyone, it is never matched against your name, your email or your Apple account, and deleting the app ends it for good.
  • Your account, only if you sign in. Sign in with Apple is optional. If you use it, we receive an identifier from Apple and, if you allow it, an email address — which may be one of Apple's private relay addresses. We do not send email to it.

Where it is kept

Your taps and your answers are stored on your iPhone. Detour works completely offline and never waits for a network to record a tap.

The anonymous usage counts above are sent to our own database, run for us by Supabase, in batches when there is a connection. They are used to answer four questions and nothing else: whether people finish setting the app up, whether they start a free trial, whether they subscribe, and whether they come back. They are not used to build a profile of you, and they are never combined with your account even if you have one.

If you sign in with Apple, a copy is also stored on our behalf by Supabase, so that your streak survives a lost or replaced phone. Rules on that database mean your rows can only be read by your own signed-in account.

What Detour does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking service. The usage counts described above go to our own database and to no one else.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and nothing that would allow it.
  • Nothing you wrote or chose is ever counted. Your reason, your plan for what to do instead, the word you use for the habit, what you spend, and what you said set off an order: none of these leaves your phone except as your own backup, if you sign in.
  • Your information is never sold, rented, or shared for anyone else's marketing.
  • No health or medical information is collected. Detour counts your own taps and makes no claim about your health.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold by Apple. Apple handles the payment and tells us only whether a subscription is active, through RevenueCat, who manage that for us. Neither Detour nor RevenueCat ever sees your card details.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled by your iPhone itself, from the times you picked. Nothing is sent from a server, and the reminders work with no network at all. You can turn them off in Detour's Settings, or in the iPhone's own Settings.

Deleting everything

In Detour, open Settings:

  • Delete account removes your account and everything stored with it, permanently, and clears this phone.
  • If you never signed in, Erase everything on this phone clears what is on the device.

Deleting the app removes the random installation identifier, so nothing further is recorded. Usage counts already recorded cannot be traced back to you to be removed, because they were never attached to anything that identifies you — that is the point of counting them the way we do. If you would rather they were deleted anyway, write to the address below with the date you installed the app and we will remove what we can find.

Deleting removes the data. We keep no backup copy of a deleted account.

Children

Detour is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes
