Privacy Policy

Detour, by C FISH ENTERPRISES LLC. Last updated 15 October 2026.

Detour is a counter. It records the times you tap a button and the answers you gave when you set it up. That is all it is for, and this page says plainly what happens to that information.

What Detour keeps

  • Your taps. The time of each craving you log, and whether you marked it beaten or a slip.
  • Your answers. How many times a week you said you eat the habit, what an order costs you, your reason, the times of day you chose, the word you use for the habit, and the date you started.
  • Your account, only if you sign in. Sign in with Apple is optional. If you use it, we receive an identifier from Apple and, if you allow it, an email address — which may be one of Apple's private relay addresses. We do not send email to it.

Where it is kept

Everything above is stored on your iPhone. Detour works completely offline and never waits for a network to record a tap.

If you sign in with Apple, a copy is also stored on our behalf by Supabase, so that your streak survives a lost or replaced phone. Rules on that database mean your rows can only be read by your own signed-in account.

What Detour does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking services.
  • Your information is never sold, rented, or shared for anyone else's marketing.
  • No health or medical information is collected. Detour counts your own taps and makes no claim about your health.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold by Apple. Apple handles the payment and tells us only whether a subscription is active, through RevenueCat, who manage that for us. Neither Detour nor RevenueCat ever sees your card details.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled by your iPhone itself, from the times you picked. Nothing is sent from a server, and the reminders work with no network at all. You can turn them off in Detour's Settings, or in the iPhone's own Settings.

Deleting everything

In Detour, open Settings:

  • Delete account removes your account and everything stored with it, permanently, and clears this phone.
  • If you never signed in, Erase everything on this phone clears what is on the device.

Deleting removes the data. We keep no backup copy of a deleted account.

Children

Detour is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
