Common questions

My day count did not go up

The count turns over at midnight, by your phone's own clock. It never resets when you record a slip.

Why did the money not change when I beat a craving?

Money kept is worked out from time, not taps: the amount you said you normally spend, spread across the days since you started. It rises once a day. Recording a slip takes one order off it.

The widget shows the wrong day

Open Detour once and it will catch up. Widgets are refreshed by iOS rather than on demand.

I am not getting reminders

Check Settings → Reminders inside Detour is switched on and has at least one time picked, then check Detour is allowed to notify you in the iPhone's own Settings → Notifications.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open the iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the renewal date stops the next charge.

How do I delete my data?

Detour → Settings → Delete account, or Erase everything on this phone if you never signed in. Both are permanent.
